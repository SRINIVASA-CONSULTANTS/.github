## About

Srinivasa Consultants brings semiconductor design verification together with research into trustworthy AI-assisted engineering. This work is informed by 18+ years of semiconductor design-verification experience.

The focus is practical: connect requirements, implementation, verification evidence, and release decisions so that engineering outcomes remain understandable and reviewable.

## Areas of focus

| Area | Focus |
| --- | --- |
| Semiconductor engineering | IP and SoC verification, interface behaviour, hardware/software integration, and verification planning. |
| AI-assisted system design | Research and development toward specification-driven IP generation, multi-IP SoC integration, and the associated software packages. |
| AI assurance | Traceable requirements, independent verification, human review, reproducible evidence, and controlled releases. |

## Current research

### Mission-to-Silicon

**Verification-Grade Assurance for AI-Assisted System Design**

Developing a governed workflow that connects system requirements to design artifacts, verification evidence, and release decisions. The intended scope includes IP generation from specifications, integration of multiple IP blocks into a SoC, and the associated software packages.

The research asks how engineering evidence and approvals should be reassessed when requirements or implementation change. These are development goals, not claims of completed or production-qualified capabilities.

### DharmaGuard

Exploratory research into values-informed AI behaviour, with an emphasis on evidence, careful interpretation, human review, and testable evaluation criteria. Safety and effectiveness claims depend on evaluation; this is not a production safety guarantee.

## Engineering principles

- **Requirements first:** implementation should be justified by an explicit requirement or an approved engineering decision.
- **Evidence before claims:** AI-generated artifacts still require appropriate verification and review.
- **Traceable change:** version-controlled work, understandable change records, and reviewable milestone releases.
